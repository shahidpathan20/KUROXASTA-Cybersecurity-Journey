Networking:

Networking is the process of connecting computers and devices so they can communicate and share data with each other.

MAIN PURPOSE OF NETWORKING .

- Share information
- Share internet connection
- Communicate between devices
- Transfer files and data

TYPES OF NETWORKING .

- <b>LAN (Local Area Network)</b> → Small network inside homes, schools, or offices
- <b>WAN (Wide Area Network)</b> → Large network connecting different locations (Example: Internet



COMMON NETWORKING DEVICES.

- <b>Router</b> → Connects networks and provides internet
- <b>Switch</b> → Connects devices inside a network
- <b>Modem</b> → Connects network to ISP/interne

EXAMPLE

When two computers connect and share files or internet, it is called networking.
