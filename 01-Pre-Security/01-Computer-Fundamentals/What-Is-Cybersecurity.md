Cybersecurity is the ongoing effort to protect individuals, organizations and governments from digital attacks by protecting networked systems and data from unauthorized use or harm.

what are the types cybersecurity

- <b>Network Security</b> : Protecting networks from attacks and unauthorized access.
- <b>Application Security:</b>  Securing apps and software from vulnerabilities.
- <b>Information Security</b> : Protecting sensitive data and information.
- <b>Cloud Security</b> :Securing cloud-based systems and data.\`
- <b>Endpoint Security:</b> Protecting devices like laptops and phones.
- <b>Offensive Security</b> :Finding weaknesses by ethical hacking.
- <b>Defensive Security</b> : Detecting and stopping cyberattacks.
- <b>Operational Security:</b> Protecting internal processes and access control
